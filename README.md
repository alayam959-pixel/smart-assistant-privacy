# Privacy Policy for Smart Assistant AI

**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

**VibeCore Studio** ("we," "our," or "us") built the **Smart Assistant AI** app as a free, ad-supported application. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Smart Assistant AI is designed with user privacy in mind. All voice notes, text summaries, and exported PDF files are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal documents or voice recordings on external servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you (such as Advertising ID and device information) to display relevant advertisements:
* [Google Play Services](https://google.com)
* [Google AdMob](https://google.com)

## 3. App Permissions
To provide core features, the app may request the following permissions on your device:
* **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** Used solely when you tap the record button to capture voice notes for transcription and summarization.
* **Storage:** Used only when you choose to export and save your summaries as PDF files on your device.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
